Get started

Ready in a minute.

  1. Install FuelCue from the Connect IQ Store and add it to a data screen of your run profile.
  2. Turn on Alerts → Connect IQ in the activity settings.
  3. Edit your schedules in the Connect IQ app: FuelCue → Settings. Schedule 1 works out of the box (6× 30 min Gel).
  4. Pick a schedule on the watch: activity menu → Connect IQ fields → FuelCue.
After your run

In Garmin Connect.

  • Charts: carbs so far and carbs per hour
  • Summary: total carbs, fuel moments, caffeine moments and schedule
  • Planned values: FuelCue reminds you, you never have to press a button
